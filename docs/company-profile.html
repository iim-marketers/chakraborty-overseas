Who we are

Chakraborty Overseas is a Kolkata-based merchant exporter of mild steel, stainless steel and high tension fasteners. We source from verified Indian manufacturing partners, check the goods against your specification, and ship with complete export documentation.

We are an exporter, not a manufacturer. We do not operate forging, plating or testing facilities of our own — the manufacturing certifications listed on this sheet belong to our sourcing partners and are declared as supplier certifications.
